## What I learned today

### GRC Fundamentals & Organizational Governance
* **Governance:** The overarching system by which an organization directs and controls its security strategy to align with business goals and risk appetite.
* **Risk Management:** The continuous process of identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating information security risks (via Mitigation, Transfer, Acceptance, or Avoidance).
* **Compliance:** Ensuring strict adherence to applicable legal standards, contractual obligations, and industry regulations to avoid legal penalties and reputational damage.

### Frameworks & Security Management Systems (SMS)
* **ISO/IEC 27001:**
  * The global standard for designing, deploying, and maintaining an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
  * Follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle for continuous security improvement.
  * Combines mandatory management clauses with technical and organizational safeguards listed in Annex A.
* **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):**
  * Practical, outcome-driven framework organized across core functions: *Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover*.
  * Used to gauge internal cybersecurity maturity and communicate risk between technical teams and executive leadership.

### Regulatory Landscape & Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)
* **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023:**
  * India's key data privacy legislation regulating the processing of digital personal data.
  * Core roles defined:
    * **Data Principal:** The individual whose personal data is collected and processed.
    * **Data Fiduciary:** The entity that determines the purpose and means of data processing.
  * Enforces purpose limitation, valid consent mechanisms, reasonable security safeguards, and mandatory incident notification to the Data Protection Board (DPB).
